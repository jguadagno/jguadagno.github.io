AI agents are changing software development far more fundamentally than IDEs, cloud computing, or DevOps ever did. As agents evolve from assistants into autonomous collaborators, engineering leaders face a new challenge: building organizations where humans and AI teammates work together effectively. Drawing on real-world experiences with GitHub Copilot, autonomous development workflows, and engineering transformation, this keynote explores what happens when every engineer gains a team of digital coworkers, the mistakes organizations make during adoption, and how leaders can prepare for the AI-native engineering organization.
